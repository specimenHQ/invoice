# INVOICE - BOOK 1

Physical board reference notes

## What The Board Gives Us

The board should function as the macro-map for prompt generation.

Reliable visible structure:

1. A 1-52 page spine.
2. Three large act zones.
3. Act One labeled around Mystery / Birth.
4. Act Two labeled around Terror / Death.
5. Act Three labeled around Action / Rebirth.
6. Large diagonal pressure lines crossing the page map.
7. A stepped/threshold structure near the end.
8. Highlighted horizontal beats that feel like discoveries, seams, or transitions.
9. A strong contrast between empty fields and dense note clusters.

Do not treat every older note on the board as a Book 1 event. The current Book 1 scope overrides it:

Book 1 is only getting out of the first crate and reaching the door.

## Act Layer For Book 1

### Act One: Mystery / Birth

Pages 1-18

Function: pre-language, pressure, body sound, growth beginning.

Visual logic: the crate is not yet recognized. The page is mostly black. Marks and punctuation are the first evidence of consciousness.

### Act Two: Terror / Death

Pages 19-38

Function: planes, corners, wood, seam, sliver, break.

Visual logic: the container becomes material. The old protection becomes unbearable. The crate begins to die as a container.

### Act Three: Action / Rebirth

Pages 39-52

Function: outside the crate, symbolic family-home dark, follow the line, reach the humming door, silhouette, first paragraph.

Visual logic: not freedom yet. Rebirth is the ability to move toward the next threshold.

## Board Geometry Rules

Use the board's physical design as composition language:

1. The page spine becomes the 52-page sequence.
2. The diagonal lines become pressure vectors inside the crate.
3. The large empty regions become blackness and not-yet-known space.
4. The dense clusters become moments of growing readability.
5. The stepped ending becomes the approach to the door and the seventh-panel breach.
6. The highlighted horizontal strokes become seams, slivers, and discovery lines.

## Prompt-Machine Use

Every generated panel should have:

1. Scene layer - what happens in the story.
2. Act layer - Mystery/Birth, Terror/Death, or Action/Rebirth.
3. Board geometry layer - empty field, diagonal pressure, dense cluster, horizontal seam, or stepped threshold.
4. Gradient layer - black to grey, never stable white.
5. Panel role - establish, disturb, answer, transform, clarify, hand-off.

This keeps the 313 panels from feeling like unrelated images.
