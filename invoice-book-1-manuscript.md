## Page 17

I stand on curved ground. Around me, walls curve upward. People walk sideways relative to me. Gravity pulls differently everywhere. I am confused. I am here. I am somewhere.

## Page 18

They handed me a robe. Gray fabric. Rough weave. I put it on. It covers me. They look at me. They measure me with instruments. I do not ask questions. Questions cost time.

## Page 19

"You are here now," one of them says. I nod. I do not speak. My voice is unused. "What is your color?" they ask. Color? I have no answer.

## Page 20

I am given a number. My robe is assigned a shade. Blue. Not deep blue. Not light. Medium blue. The average. I eat bland food. I sleep in allocated beds. Work is scheduled.

## Page 21

"Welcome," he says. The man wears red. "What did you wake from?" "Darkness," I say. My voice is cracked. "I don't remember." He nods. "Most don't. That's how it starts. Welcome to the Sphere."

## Page 22

I eat. I work. I sleep. Repeat. Days pass. I count them by the light cycles overhead. Twenty times up, twenty times down. Friends develop. We laugh at jokes I barely understand. The sword under my pillow hums. I pretend it does not.

## Page 23

Sometimes I remember the hum. The music point in the dark. I touch the blanket where the vibration lives. It is still there. Fainter than before. But present. The people around me do not hear it. Only I hear it. This must be mine alone. I keep it secret.

## Page 24

The first memory speaks to me at night. Not aloud. Inside. A child's voice. Young. Confident. "You left me there." I wake sweating. The room is silent. The other sleepers breathe evenly. Did anyone else hear it? I check. No one speaks. I pretend I did not hear.

## Page 25

Who is the child? Mine? Someone else's? Universal? I have no children. I have no family. I have only the void and the sphere. But the voice knows me. It says "me" as if we are connected. Are we? I don't know anymore.

## Page 26

I think about leaving. The thought is dangerous. I hide it. I look at the Cube door. I remember opening it. I remember the white. I remember the fall. I could go back. Could I? Would I survive? The hum gets louder. It tells me. "Go." I hesitate.

## Page 27

The friends notice my absences. They watch me drift at meals. They ask what is wrong. I lie. "Nothing," I say. "Just tired." They believe me. They trust me. That trust becomes heavier than the lie. I deserve it. I do not deserve it. I carry both.

## Page 28

Pack. The sword. Wrap it. Hide it. Take the egg (the flashlight). Fold the robe. Leave the number. I walk to the edge. I look at the Sphere. I look at the Cube. The choice is mine. Not theirs. Mine. I take the step.

## Page 29

"You're going," she says. Her name is Maya. She wears yellow. She knows. "Yes," I say. "I have to." She doesn't try to stop me. She doesn't need to. Some roads are lonely by design. I hug her. I thank her. I leave.

## Page 30

Walk. Step. Breathe. The path darkens. The Sphere fades behind me. Lights shrink to dots. Dots to nothing. I am alone again. The hum is my only companion. It guides. It warns. It waits. I follow. I follow. I follow.

## Page 31

The road curves. The sky darkens. I can see my breath. Cold returns. Familiar. The hum grows stronger. It vibrates in my chest. Not noise now. Voice. "Keep going," it says. I keep going.

## Page 38

I am in the warehouse. Crates surround me. Thousands. Millions. The darkness is thick. My breath shows. The hum settles. The sword wakes. It is time.

## Page 39

I approach the first crate. It sits alone. I reach out. I open it. The lid creaks. Something moves inside. "Hello," I say. No answer. "Are you in there?" Silence. Then: "Yes." It is a voice. Small.

## Page 40

"Don't hurt me."

"Why did you come?"
"To find you."
"Why now?"
"Because I had to."
"Okay. I'll help."

## Page 41

The memory steps out. It glows faintly. Golden edges. Not threatening. It is what I feared. What I hoped. What I buried. It looks at me. I look at it. Neither of us speaks. We understand. The first bond forms.

## Page 42

It moves toward a second crate. I follow. It touches the lid. The lock clicks open. This one next, it says. Its voice is not mine. It is younger. Pure. Unbroken. I let it lead. I trust it now.

## Page 43

Third crate. I open it too fast.
The memory lunges.
Agitated.
Jagged.
Dark sparks.
Instinct: strike.
Sword rises.
I swing.
Connection made.
Memory shatters.
Silence falls.

## Page 44

Then the wall cracks. Dust falls. Light dims across the warehouse. The cube groans. I feel it in my bones. What have I done? I broke it. I broke the memory. I broke the container. I broke myself.

## Page 45

I drop the sword. I kneel. I touch the crack. It is still warm. The damage is real. I understand now. Violence against memory damages reality. I cannot do this again. Never again.

## Page 46

To the memory that died: I am sorry. To the cube: I am sorry. To myself: I am sorry. I will not make this mistake again. I will learn. I will heal. I will try.

## Page 47

Fourth crate. I sit. I wait. I listen. "I'm afraid," the voice says. "So am I," I answer. The lid opens gently. This memory steps out calm. Stable. Warm. This is how it works. Not force. Connection. Trust. Naming. Holding.

## Page 48

Memory joins me. Another follows. Another. We move as one. The sword grows heavier. Not burden. Weight. Presence. I carry them. They carry me. We are together. We are whole. We are ready.

## Page 49

The following walks with me. Through aisles of crates. Through darkness. Through fear. The child memory leads. Warriors guard the rear. Helpers open what I cannot. I watch. I learn. I listen. I trust.

## Page 50

Round and round we go. Crate after crate. Memory after memory. Each one integrated. Each one released. Each one part. The sword hums steady. The child sings soft. The dark is no longer dark.

## Page 51

Now I walk.
I look back once.
I turn forward now.
The choice is made. The path is chosen. The invoice continues.

## Page 52

INVOICE
Behind me lies the Sphere. Friends. Safety. Warmth. I remember them. I will miss them. They are good people. I am leaving them. That is hard. But necessary.
Ahead lies the Tree. Truth. Pain. Growth. I feel it calling. I feel it waiting. I am going toward it. I am choosing. I am committing. I am stepping. Into dark. Into self. Into home.
